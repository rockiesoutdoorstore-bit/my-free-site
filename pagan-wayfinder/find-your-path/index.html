Find Your Path

A 12-question reflection to help surface your strongest interests. Results are invitations to explore — never diagnoses or fixed identities.

Loading quiz…

How this works

Each answer contributes points to different interest areas (nature, ritual, mythology, history, magic, deity relationships, divination, seasonal practice, and specific cultural affinities). At the end, those scores are mapped to the traditions on this site and the top three matches are shown with short explanations and links.

You can retake the quiz as many times as you like. Your answers stay only in your browser — nothing is stored on a server.
