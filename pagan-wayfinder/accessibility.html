Accessibility

We want Pagan Wayfinder to be usable by as many people as possible. The site is built with semantic HTML, responsive design, and sufficient color contrast. Navigation is keyboard-accessible, and the mobile menu can be operated with a keyboard or screen reader.

If you encounter barriers — whether related to vision, motor control, cognitive load, or anything else — please let us know via the Contact page. We will do our best to improve the experience.

Future improvements may include better ARIA labeling, skip links, and alternative formats for longer content.
