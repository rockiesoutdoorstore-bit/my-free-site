Reading List

A starting selection of books. This is not exhaustive and will grow over time. Prioritize what genuinely interests you.

General Introductions

  • The Triumph of the Moon — Ronald Hutton (history of modern pagan witchcraft)
  • Paganism: An Introduction to Earth-Centered Religions — Joyce & River Higginbotham
  • The Earth Path — Starhawk (nature-centered practice)

Wicca & Witchcraft

  • Witchcraft Today — Gerald Gardner (historical primary text)
  • The Spiral Dance — Starhawk
  • Traditional Wicca: A Seeker’s Guide — Thorn Mooney

Druidry & Celtic

  • The Druidry Handbook — John Michael Greer
  • Celtic Myths and Legends — various scholarly collections

Heathenry / Norse

  • The Poetic Edda (translation of your choice — Larrington, Orchard, etc.)
  • A Practical Heathen’s Guide to Asatru — Patricia M. Lafayllve
  • Works by scholars such as H.R. Ellis Davidson for historical context

Hellenic

  • Primary sources: Homer, Hesiod, Homeric Hymns
  • Modern practice guides from reconstructionist authors (seek current recommendations)

A note on reading

Academic and historical works help you understand context. Practice-oriented books help you experiment. Both are valuable. Be cautious of any source that claims absolute authority or demands expensive courses as the only valid path.
