Privacy Policy

Pagan Wayfinder is currently a static website. We do not operate user accounts, and the Path Quiz processes answers entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to a server or stored by us.

If we later add analytics, email signup, or other features that collect data, this page will be updated with clear details about what is collected, why, and how it is used.

Third-party hosting or content-delivery services may log standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) according to their own policies.
