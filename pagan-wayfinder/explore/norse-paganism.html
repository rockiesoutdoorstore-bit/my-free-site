Norse Paganism

Modern spiritual engagement with the gods, myths, cosmology, and cultural heritage of the Norse and wider Germanic world.

Relationship to Heathenry

Norse Paganism and Heathenry overlap heavily. “Norse Paganism” often highlights the specifically Scandinavian mythological corpus (the Eddas, sagas, and related material), while “Heathenry” can be a broader term covering Anglo-Saxon, continental Germanic, and Norse streams. Many people use the terms interchangeably or prefer one over the other for cultural or personal reasons.

Central Themes

  • The Aesir and Vanir gods and goddesses
  • Cosmology (Yggdrasil, the Nine Worlds)
  • Concepts of fate, honor, and reciprocal relationships
  • Runes as both historical writing system and modern divinatory/magical tool
  • Ancestor veneration and connection to cultural heritage

Getting Started

Reliable entry points include readable translations of the Poetic and Prose Eddas, accessible historical overviews, and contemporary authors who combine scholarship with lived practice. As with Heathenry, be mindful of the political and ethical diversity within the broader community.
