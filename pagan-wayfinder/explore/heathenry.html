Heathenry

Modern revival and reconstruction of the pre-Christian religions of the Germanic-speaking peoples of Northern Europe.

What is Heathenry?

Heathenry (also called Ásatrú, Forn Sed, or Germanic polytheism) refers to contemporary practice inspired by the religious traditions of the Anglo-Saxons, Norse, continental Germanic peoples, and related cultures before Christianization. Practitioners honor gods such as Odin, Thor, Freyja, Frigg, and many others, as well as ancestors and land spirits (landvættir).

Key Elements

  • Polytheistic devotion to the Norse/Germanic gods and goddesses
  • Emphasis on reciprocity (gift-giving, hospitality, honor)
  • Blót (ritual offerings) and sumbel (ritual toasting)
  • Interest in historical sources: Eddas, sagas, archaeology, folklore
  • Varied approaches — from highly reconstructionist to more modern and inclusive

Important Note

Heathenry is practiced by a wide range of people. Some groups have unfortunately been associated with exclusionary or racist ideologies. Many Heathens and Heathen organizations explicitly reject racism and white supremacy and work to keep the tradition open and ethical. When exploring, look for groups and authors who clearly state inclusive values.
