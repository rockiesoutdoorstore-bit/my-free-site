About Pagan Wayfinder

A free, independent educational resource for people exploring modern paganism and earth-centered spirituality.

Our purpose

Pagan Wayfinder exists to offer clear, respectful, and accessible information about the many paths that fall under the broad umbrella of modern paganism. We aim to reduce confusion for newcomers, provide useful orientation for those already exploring, and model a non-dogmatic, non-gatekeeping approach.

What we are not

We are not a religious organization, initiatory order, or authoritative voice for any tradition. We do not claim to speak for practitioners of any specific path. Content is educational and exploratory.

Values

  • Clarity over mystification
  • Respect for both scholarly and lived approaches
  • Inclusivity and rejection of racism, bigotry, and exploitation
  • Transparency about sources and limitations
  • Encouragement of personal discernment

Questions, corrections, or suggestions are welcome via the Contact page.
